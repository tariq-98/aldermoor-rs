# Aldermoor

A real-time fantasy RPG for Apple-silicon Macs, rebuilt in Rust: keyboard movement, an adjustable
third-person camera, and a Fable, World of Warcraft and Guild Wars feel, with visuals as close to
AAA as an M1 Air allows.

These pages are published from the [`wiki/`](https://github.com/tariq-98/aldermoor-rs/tree/main/wiki)
folder in the repo. Edit them there through a PR; edits made on the wiki itself are overwritten.
