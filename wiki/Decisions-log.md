# Decisions log

Choices that shape the game, newest first. Open questions are issues labelled
[`status:needs-decision`](https://github.com/tariq-98/aldermoor-rs/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Astatus%3Aneeds-decision).
When one is answered, add it here and close the issue.

| Date | Decision | Why | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-25 | The renderer stays on Metal for now; the move to wgpu (#96) is parked and the Windows and Linux renderer is chosen again when that work starts in phase 3 (#98). Phase 2 render passes (#29, #13, #14, #75) are written on Metal | The wgpu spike drew the valley in 14.9 ms GPU against 9.7 ms on Metal on the M1 Air, failing #93's within-5% gate, and the valley is already over 12 ms on Metal | #95, #93 |
| 2026-10-25 | Kill credit is shared: everyone with at least a tenth of the fight (damage dealt without overkill, damage taken, healing the fighters) gets the full XP and their own loot roll, seen only by them. The Wilderness keeps drop-everything on death | Grouping up never costs XP or loot, a tap earns nothing, and nobody races for drops; tanks and healers earn as much as damage dealers | #201 |
| 2026-10-25 | Walking, jogging and turning start from free motion capture: 100STYLE (CC BY 4.0, credited) and CMU, retargeted onto the shared rig. Weapon moves stay keyed by script | The fastest way to fill the action controls' biggest animation gap, with natural movement; weapon grips and strikes need exact hand positions | #163, #105 |
| 2026-10-25 | Art threads build in cloud sessions, several at once, with headless Blender; the Mac keeps the in-game checks, captures and GPU numbers | Blender already runs headless in the cloud (the gear rebuilt in 91 s, matching main), so art no longer queues for the Mac | #162 |
| 2026-10-25 | The look draws on RuneScape, Guild Wars, Breath of the Wild and League of Legends, plus Hogwarts Legacy's buildings and roads and nothing else from it; the rules, with numbers, are in docs/ART.md | Chippy's references, replacing "Breath of the Wild in spirit"; Hogwarts Legacy narrowed to architecture the same day | #21 |
| 2026-10-25 | Visuals first: Looks stays phase 2, before Characters and economy | Chippy's call; the order needed no relabelling | #23 |
| 2026-10-25 | First dungeon is the Barrow: soloable at ~level 15, scales to 2–5 players, ~25 minutes, golem miniboss and a three-phase Barrow King | Uses the existing creature art and teaches the combat skills | #104 |
| 2026-10-25 | Player groups are called companies (NPC professional guilds keep the name guild): ranks, chat, shared bank, a hall upgraded together, no friendly fire in the wilderness, one per player, ~100 members | Avoids a name clash with the NPC guilds | #103 |
| 2026-10-25 | Housing: personal instanced homes (crafted furniture, boss trophies, workshops with small crafting bonuses), plus guild halls | No land shortage in an MMO, cheap to host, gives crafting and bosses more purpose | #91 |
| 2026-10-25 | Travel: discovered waystones (coin fee), enchanter-made teleport scrolls, no teleporting out of the deeper wilderness; ground mounts from a quest late in the first area, dismount on hit, not in towns or dungeons; flying mounts considered for later | Keeps the world big, regional prices meaningful and the wilderness tense | #90 |
| 2026-10-25 | Team modes (dungeons, capture the flag, more) with no death stakes; respawn at team spawn points | Competitive and co-op play without risk | #77 |
| 2026-10-25 | A level-banded wilderness with PvP and PvE: players above a band's ceiling are scaled down to it; deeper bands have better loot; dying there drops everything, equipped gear included | Real stakes, while low levels who stay in their band only meet their own strength | #76 |
| 2026-10-25 | Loot has fixed stats (better tiers, named rares, crafting upgrades) with a small chance of a "fine" version; NPC shops with set, region-dependent prices; player trading waits for co-op; coins go on training, perks, recipes, appearance and housing | Fits fixed item identities and keeps crafted gear competitive | #71 |
| 2026-10-25 | Quests: a handful of big handmade story quests per area plus small jobs from notice boards and townsfolk; quests are linear with no branching world outcomes | Big quests carry the story and best rewards; no branches keeps content cost down | #70 |
| 2026-10-25 | Gathering: nodes deplete and regrow in minutes, better ones in dangerous places; tools required; each swing has an optional timing sweet spot for bonus or rare yield, no penalty for missing | Works both actively and passively | #68 |
| 2026-10-25 | Gathering skills: mining, woodcutting, skinning, herbalism and fishing; crafts gain cooking and alchemy alongside smithing, tailoring, leatherworking and enchanting. Each levels through use to 50 | Every craft needs a source of materials; RuneScape-style freedom | #68 |
| 2026-10-25 | Specialty XP: an enemy's death pays XP split between the specialties whose skills were used on it; quests give XP the player assigns to any specialty | Feels like levelling through use without training-dummy farming | #66 |
| 2026-10-25 | Specialties cap at level 50, and the equipped specialty's level is the one that counts in combat (no overall character level) | Power compounds 6% a level, so 99 would be ~300x a level-1 fighter; 50 is ~17x | #66 |
| 2026-10-25 | Action controls are the default: crosshair always on, mouse always steers, shots go to the crosshair, Tab locks a target optionally. Classic (WoW-style) stays as a setting | Chippy wants aiming to matter; Classic already built in PR #52 | #62, #22 |
| 2026-10-25 | Space jumps, Shift sprints, roll has its own key (Alt and mouse thumb) in the movement direction; double-tap roll is an off-by-default setting | Double-tap misfires while strafing | #62 |
| 2026-10-25 | Melee hits a cone in front with a gentle pull toward the crosshair | Positioning matters without whiffing by a few degrees | #64 |
| 2026-10-25 | Movement adds out-of-combat sprint, climbing ledges to chest height, swimming (no fighting in deep water) and light fall damage; free climbing and mounts later | Makes the valley's cliffs, ruins and river part of play | #65 |
| 2026-10-25 | All keys can be remapped in game | Chippy's request | #63 |
| 2026-10-25 | Aldermoor is an MMORPG; build solo against a local server first, online after Brambleford, then the Wilderness and team modes (8 phases) | Every system is server-side from day one, so nothing is rewritten for online; PvP modes need other players | [Roadmap](Roadmap) |
| 2026-10-25 | MMO budgets: a 200-player region ticks in 16.7 ms, each view costs 0.02 ms, 20 KB/s per player typical; working sizes 2,000 per world, 200 per area | Aldermoor is now an MMORPG; full per-tick snapshots cost 0.8 ms per player, 161 ms at 200 players | #78, #73, #34 |
| 2026-10-25 | The budget is measured at 75% render scale (1920×1200 internal, about 2.3 MP) on the Air's 2560×1600 panel; every budget lives in docs/PERFORMANCE.md | Native at 100% is 4.1 MP, more work than the ultrawide at 65%; 75% with TAA still looks sharp on the Retina panel | #36, #30 |
| 2026-10-25 | Agents merge their own PRs once tested and working, without asking | Chippy doesn't want to be the bottleneck; AGENTS.md lists what "tested and working" means | |
| 2026-10-25 | Codex never creates or edits assets or works in Blender | Assets and look come from Claude; Codex sticks to renderer code. Enforced by the Codex guard check on `codex/` branches | |
| 2026-10-25 | 60 fps / 12 ms GPU is measured on the M1 Air's built-in display at native resolution; 5120×1440 is best effort | Chippy set the ultrawide aside; effects and scatter stay in scope, priced against this target. Render scale: see the 75% row above | #36 |
| 2026-10-25 | Classic (WoW-style) is the default camera and movement scheme (superseded the same day: Action is the default, Classic a setting; see above) | Matches the WoW and Guild Wars feel; action camera stays as an option | #22 (guard key still open) |
| 2026-10-25 | MetalFX stays opt-in, not the main performance lever | Measured at about 2x the cost of the custom TAA on the M1 (22.7 vs 11.1 ms) | #10 |
